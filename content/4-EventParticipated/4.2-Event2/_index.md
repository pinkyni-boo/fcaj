### Event objective

I attended this event to learn not only AWS-related knowledge but also better teamwork and product-development habits. Since my work in CloudDoc involves building the user-facing flow, I wanted to understand how confidence, scope control, and DevOps thinking affect the quality of collaborative software projects.

### Speaker

The speakers came from community and practitioner backgrounds, and their presentations combined technical topics with personal development, teamwork, and real project lessons. This mix made the event especially relevant for students and interns.

### Detailed content

One major theme was learning AWS safely and effectively. The speakers talked about how beginners often hesitate because they fear making mistakes or creating unexpected cost. That was highly relevant to our team because CloudDoc also depends on cloud resources, so careful experimentation matters.

Another strong part of the event was the discussion about procrastination, confidence, and teamwork under pressure. The speakers explained that growth in technology is often blocked not by lack of intelligence but by hesitation, unclear priorities, and weak coordination. I connected this directly to frontend work, where frequent iterations and clear communication are necessary to keep the product moving.

The DevOps discussion also left a strong impression on me. It highlighted that many visible technical problems are actually process problems underneath. That idea helped me understand why frontend and backend integration should be aligned early instead of being treated as separate final-stage tasks.

### Experience and reflection

This event helped me become more disciplined in how I approach product work. Instead of trying to make every screen perfect immediately, I started to value clearer priorities, faster validation, and closer coordination with the backend side. For CloudDoc, this mindset is useful because user experience improves more when the core flow is stable than when the interface only looks polished on the surface.

### Conclusion

The event was valuable because it balanced technology with mindset and collaboration. For my role in CloudDoc, the most important takeaway was that good frontend output depends heavily on communication, scope control, and early integration thinking.
