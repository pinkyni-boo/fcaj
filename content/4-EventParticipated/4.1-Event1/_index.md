# Event 1

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** Workshop: Enterprise AI Assistants and AWS Infrastructure Audit Automation with GenAI
- **Date & Time:** 09:00, May 23, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee

### Summary of Content and Main Activities

- **Overview of Agentic AI:** Analyzed the everyday pain points of enterprise users who repeatedly perform manual tasks, then introduced AI assistant systems such as Amazon Q as a way to optimize workflows.
- **Connectivity and integration capabilities:** Showed how AI can connect to an organization’s knowledge base through more than 40 data connectors, supporting retrieval from databases, data warehouses, and uploaded files.
- **Practical use case demonstrations:** Featured demos of business automation use cases, such as a project management assistant that can summarize meeting minutes, send notification emails, and schedule follow-up meetings.
- **Automated auditing with GenAI:** Presented a generative AI approach to automating AWS workload auditing while maintaining compliance with strict security standards.

### Outcomes and Value Gained

- **Lessons learned:** Realized the importance of a unified working environment where humans and AI agents can collaborate to shorten the time from insight generation to action.
- **New skills:** Expanded understanding of responsible AI practices in enterprises, including guardrails, secure data governance, and compliance requirements.

### Skills and Experience Accumulated

- **Business process automation mindset:** Learned how AI can be applied to solve bottlenecks in administrative work and project management.
- **Cloud security and audit knowledge:** Gained perspective on how Bedrock models can be used to review and assess the safety of AWS network infrastructure.
