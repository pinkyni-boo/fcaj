During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** from **17/04/2026** to **10/07/2026**, I had the opportunity to learn in a cloud-oriented environment and contribute directly to the **CloudDoc** project. My main role was frontend development, but the project also pushed me to understand backend integration, metadata structure, and AWS-oriented system thinking more deeply.

Looking back, I can see that I improved in ways that go beyond interface building. At the beginning, I was most confident in UI implementation and component organization. After working on CloudDoc, I learned that strong frontend work depends on data flow, business logic alignment, and system architecture awareness. That shift in mindset was one of the most meaningful outcomes of my internship.

### Areas where I improved the most

- User-interface organization for multi-step product flows.
- Translating technical requirements into practical implementation tasks.
- Collaborating with backend instead of treating frontend work as isolated.
- Self-learning through documentation, workshops, and project problem-solving.

### Main strengths

**1. Proactive working style**

I was proactive in handling key frontend areas such as document search, upload flow, preview screens, and admin-facing pages. When integration details were unclear, I usually tried to understand the problem first before asking for support.

**2. Fast learning and adaptability**

During the internship, I had to work with topics that went beyond pure frontend, including presigned URLs, metadata structures, S3 upload flow, and architecture thinking. Even though these were not my strongest areas at first, I was able to learn quickly and connect them to my work.

**3. Team collaboration**

I collaborated well with teammates, especially in areas where frontend decisions depended on API design, metadata, and moderation flow. This helped the project move more consistently across roles.

### Areas to improve

**1. Production-level system thinking**

Although I now understand architecture and integration better, I still need to improve my production-level system thinking, especially around logging, monitoring, and complex deployment-time failures.

**2. Technical communication**

I want to become more confident in presenting technical solutions in a concise and structured way. There were times when I understood the solution but did not express it clearly enough to others.

**3. Deeper hands-on AWS experience**

I gained useful exposure to AWS-related architecture and integration concepts, but I still need more experience with fully configured cloud environments to grow stronger in deployment and operations.

### Personal reflection in structured format

**Difficulty**

My biggest challenge was moving from UI work based on mock data to UI work that had to follow the real system data flow. As I worked deeper on CloudDoc, I had to understand metadata, presigned URLs, document approval states, and backend constraints, while my original strength was mainly interface implementation.

**How I addressed it**

I handled this by breaking the problem into smaller parts: reviewing the business flow, identifying the input and output data for each screen, clarifying API contracts with the backend side, and testing each connection step by step instead of trying to understand the whole system at once. At the same time, I used workshops, technical blogs, and AWS documentation to close the knowledge gaps.

**Future direction**

After this internship, I want to keep improving in three directions: stronger frontend product-building skills, better backend integration understanding, and more hands-on AWS experience in deployment, monitoring, and cost optimization. I also want to improve the way I present technical solutions so I can communicate more clearly in team settings.

### Overall reflection

Overall, I believe I performed well in the role assigned to me and showed clear growth in both technical skills and working mindset. More importantly, I no longer see frontend as a separate area. I now understand it as one part of a larger product and system. That is a very important foundation for my future development.
